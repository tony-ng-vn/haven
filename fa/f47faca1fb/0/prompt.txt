not copy the movie literally, but borrow these feelings:

### 1. a sense of invisible connection

the user should feel like they are not alone in an empty app
they are entering a world already filled with soft lights, and they are becoming one of them

### 2. softness and mystery

### 3. memory leaving traces

their name, photo, interests, city — each one should “build” their presence

### 4. emotional quiet

*Your Name* is magical, but not loud
the experience should feel intimate, calm, and slightly dreamy

# visual direction

## color mood

think:

* deep twilight blue
* soft indigo
* warm pink/orange glow
* faint gold light
* a little lavender

basically **evening sky + city lights + comet glow**

## shapes

* floating particles
* glowing threads
* soft blurred orbs
* glassy cards
* circular or rounded containers
* gentle gradients

## motion

motion matters a lot here

use:

* slow drifting particles
* subtle parallax
* lines of light forming between points
* little glow pulses when user completes a step
* transitions that feel like “sliding through memory” instead of hard cuts

avoid:

* bouncy startup-app animations
* loud celebration confetti
* overly playful gamified motion

# how to turn profile setup into a magical experience

instead of plain fields, make each answer **manifest** into the world.

## example onboarding flow

### screen 1 — opening mood

dark, dreamy background with scattered distant lights

copy:

> every connection begins somewhere.
> some stay for a moment. some stay with us.

button:
**enter haven**

---

### screen 2 — name

prompt:

> what should people call you?

when they type their name, it appears as a glowing label or softly illuminated signature in the scene

feels like:
they are not entering text
they are **placing themselves into the world**

---

### screen 3 — photo / avatar

prompt:

> how should people recognize you?

after upload, their image appears inside a glowing frame or orb
maybe a little ring of light forms around it

feels like:
their identity is taking shape

---

### screen 4 — location / world

prompt:

> where are you in the world right now?

instead of a boring location field, maybe a tiny constellation point appears on a map-like glow field
or a little light settles into the background landscape

feels like:
they are locating themselves in a wider human network

---

### screen 5 — interests / conversation starters

prompt:

> what feels like you?
> or
> what could someone talk to you about for hours?

each selected interest becomes a little floating object, icon, or memory fragment around their space

for example:

* music → glowing note
* books → small floating book
* coding → tiny pixel star / terminal spark
* coffee → warm cup glow

feels like:
they are building their personal atmosphere

---

### screen 6 — belonging reveal

after the last step, zoom out a bit and show their full “presence” now formed:

* name
* photo
* a few interest fragments
* their little light in the Haven world
* maybe faint nearby lights hinting at future connections

copy:

> your place in Haven is ready.

button:
**continue**

that final moment is where the belonging feeling lands

---

# design principle to keep in mind

the core trick is:

> **every input should create a visible emotional result**

if the user answers something and the UI looks basically the same, it will still feel like a form.

if each answer changes the space around them, it starts feeling magical.

---

# copy tone

if you want *Your Name* energy, the copy should be:

* short
* soft
* slightly poetic
* but still clear

good:

* what should people call you?
* how should people recognize you?
* where are you these days?
* what feels like you?
* your place is ready.

not good:

* please complete your personal profile information
* enter your full legal name
* select three interests from the categories below
