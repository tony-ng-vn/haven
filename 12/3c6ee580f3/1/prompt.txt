**Primary hypothesis (best supported by the evidence collected):** Safari's system "Share..." action for a web page does not hand the share sheet a single `public.url` attachment the way `ShareLink(item: URL)` does. It conventionally bundles the URL together with at least one additional `NSItemProvider` attachment in the same extension item -- typically the page title/selection as `public.plain-text`, and often a page thumbnail image as well.
