Haven iOS - sky rendering, round 2

Where does the figure live when a question already owns the top?

Round 1 asked how to scale the sky and got the wrong answer, because scaling was never the problem. placeMajors caps every star at 62% of the sky's own height, so the figure occupies the top 62% of whatever region it is given. Give it the screen and it lands on the rows. Give it a taller source and it still lands on the rows.

Decision 7 says "figure owns the top, serif name below it". That describes the card reveal, which has no header. Screen 1 also says committing your name lights the first star, so the figure is live during onboarding too. Nobody ever decided what happens when a question and the figure both want the top. That is the real gap.

Each variant answers it differently. The figure region is measured from where the content actually is, not hardcoded, so it adapts as you switch screens. The panel counts how many figure stars land on any text, headline included. That last part matters: a variant can clear the rows and still sit on the question.
