Haven iOS - sky rendering, round 1

The sky was authored for a 384x560 card. The screen is 393x852. What should give?

Every number in SkyGenerator was tuned in a 384x560 space, which is a card shape. A phone screen is much taller and narrower in proportion, so filling it means scaling by 1.52x and cropping a third of the width away. The nebulae are radial gradients that are brightest at their centres, so cropping to the middle shows their cores rather than their soft edges.

These are real generated skies, not mockups: the generator below is the same FNV-1a hash, mulberry32 stream and Prim's tree as src/sky.ts and the Swift port. Switch seeds to check a decision against more than one person's sky.
