# 5 Off-Brand Treatments for the "Person from a Screenshot" Card

Note on fonts: where a brand's real typeface is proprietary (CashMarket, Mabry, Monzo Sans, Circular/Spotify Mix), I give you the closest loadable Google Fonts substitute so you can actually ship the look.

---

## 1. HOLOGRAPHIC TRADING CARD

**(a) References + the specific detail to steal**
- **Pokemon TCG holofoil** -- the foil lives only inside the art box on a standard holo, while text/frame stay matte; reverse-holo flips that (foil everywhere except the art box). Steal this: put the shimmer ONLY on the screenshot, keep the bio/handle rows matte. Modern Special-Illustration-Rare cards add physical ridges via hot-foil stamping (debossed texture), and secret rares use gold-sparkle textured starbursts. (Bulbapedia Holofoil; CGC rarity guide)
- **poke-holo.simey.me** -- the canonical CSS recreation of the tilt effect: a `background` sandwich of a repeating linear-gradient color-band + a "sparkle" mask, driven by pointer position, composited with `mix-blend-mode: color-dodge` / `overlay` and a moving `background-position`. This is your exact motion recipe.
- **Panini / Prizm** die-cut chrome frames and **NBA Top Shot** slick black moment cards -- steal the ornate metallic border + a corner rarity gem and a stat badge row.

**(b) Signature colors**
- Iridescent sweep across the classic foil hues: magenta `#FF3CAC`, cyan `#2EE6D6`, violet `#7A5CFF`, lime `#B6FF3C`, gold `#FFD447` -- run them as an angled repeating gradient.
- Card base: near-black `#0B0B12` or pearl white `#F4F1E9`; gold frame `#C9A227`.

**(c) Fonts (Google Fonts)**
- Ornate name plate: **Cinzel** (Trajan-style engraved caps) or **Cormorant** for a classier die-cut look.
- Stat badges / HP-style numerals: **Anton** or **Archivo Black** (fat condensed, reads as "sports card").
- Body/handle: **Oswald** condensed.

**(d) Signature devices**
1. Pointer-driven holographic sweep on the screenshot only (`color-dodge` blend + moving gradient), glare that tracks tilt.
2. Corner **rarity gem** (faceted diamond) + a small foil rarity star (Pokemon's black star).
3. Ornate metallic die-cut border with a **stat badge strip** (platform icon, follower count, "first met" date styled like HP/attack rows).

## 2. INSTANT FILM / POLAROID SCRAPBOOK

**(a) References + the specific detail to steal**
- **Polaroid / i-Type frame geometry** -- exact proportions: outer 4.2 x 3.5 in, square image 3.1 x 3.1 in, and the bottom border is intentionally wider than the top and sides. Steal this exactly: square-crop the screenshot, thick white frame, oversized bottom lip for the handwritten caption.
- **Fujifilm Instax** -- slightly cooler, glossier white frame, smaller square image; good if you want a tighter, more modern instant look.
- **Dazz Cam / Huji** retro-camera apps -- steal the film treatment on the photo: warm cast, faint grain, date stamp in the corner, mild light leak.
- **Corkboard + washi tape scrapbooks** -- steal the tape strips pinning the photo down and the slight rotation.

**(b) Signature colors**
- Frame: warm off-white `#FAF6EE` / `#F5F0E6` (never pure #FFF).
- Film cast on the photo: sepia warm `#C8A06A`, faded teal shadow `#7A9E9A` (from the documented Polaroid palette `#daede8 #bfd5cb #abc9cf`).
- Ink for captions: faded ballpoint blue `#2E3A59` or pencil grey `#4A4A4A`.
- Washi tape: muted pastel `#F2C6C2`, `#BFD8C4`, semi-transparent.

**(c) Fonts (Google Fonts)**
- Handwritten caption: **Caveat** (the most authentic everyday-handwriting sim on GF) or **Shadows Into Light** for a neater journal hand; **Gochi Hand** / **Kalam** as alts.
- Date-stamp: **Cutive Mono** or **Courier Prime** (typewriter/film-counter feel).

**(d) Signature devices**
1. Thick asymmetric film frame (fat bottom border) + slight `rotate(-2deg)` and a soft drop shadow so it sits ON the page.
2. Two **washi-tape strips** (semi-transparent, torn ends) pinning the top corners.
3. Film treatment on the photo: warm grain overlay + tiny corner **date stamp** in mono, optional light leak.

## 3. POP DRENCH / YEAR-IN-REVIEW

**(a) References + the specific detail to steal**
- **Cash App** -- one flat, fully-drenched saturated background, minimal chrome, the brand color owns the whole surface. Steal: single loud background, everything else reads on top of it.
- **Spotify Wrapped 2024** -- "type as form": oversized number/name shapes with gradients poured into the letterforms, dramatic blood-red / neon-pink / canary-yellow combos. Steal: make the person's NAME the hero at display size, gradient-filled.
- **Gumroad** -- flat bubblegum-pink canvas, chunky black outlines, playful. Steal the hard black borders + drop-shadow "sticker" buttons on a candy field.
- **Monzo Hot Coral** -- one confident coral owns the identity. **Poolsuite** -- retro-sunset gradient maximalism.

**(b) Signature colors (real brand hexes)**
- Cash App green `#00D632`.
- Gumroad pink `#FF90E8`.
- Monzo Hot Coral `#FF4D56` (Monzo's own stated value; some sources `#FF4B60`).
- Spotify Wrapped 2024 trio: blood red, neon pink, canary yellow -- pair e.g. `#E90B2C` + `#FF4FA3` + `#FFE14D`.
- Pick ONE as the drench; use a second only for the oversized type.

**(c) Fonts (Google Fonts)**
- Oversized hero name: **Bricolage Grotesque** (display-y, characterful) or **Archivo Black** / **Anton** for maximum shout.
- The Cash App "wide" fintech feel (real font Agrandir Wide is proprietary): use **Anybody** (variable width, crank the width axis) or **Archivo Expanded**.
- Body/handle: **Space Grotesk** or **Hanken Grotesk**.

**(d) Signature devices**
1. Full-bleed single saturated drench; the screenshot sits in a rounded "sticker" tile with a hard offset shadow.
2. Oversized **gradient-filled name** that breaks the grid (Wrapped-style type-as-form), possibly clipping the card edge.
3. Chunky playful chips for platform/@handle (pill badges, hard black outline a la Gumroad), optional slight tilt.

## 4. TERMINAL SCAN HUD

**(a) References + the specific detail to steal**
- **Blade Runner Voight-Kampff machine** -- CRT readout aesthetic, hairline geometric-sans logotype with very tight kerning (Art Deco lean), and the "machine reading a person" framing with the razor-thin eye-scan. Steal: treat the screenshot as a subject being scanned, with a reticle over the eye/face.
- **Cyberpunk HUD / hacker terminals (VT323 lineage)** -- phosphor glow, horizontal scanlines, the CRT beam-smear. Steal the scanline overlay + green/amber phosphor text.
- **Arc / Linear extreme-dark UI** -- for the RESTRAINT version: near-black surface, one hairline grid, one accent, precise mono readout rows instead of full retro camp.

**(b) Signature colors**
- Surface: near-black `#0A0E0A` / `#0D0F14`.
- Phosphor green `#33FF66` / `#00FF9C`; amber alt `#FFB000`.
- Danger/glitch accent: `#FF3B3B` and cyan `#22D3EE`.
- Dim grid lines: `#1E2A22` at low opacity.

**(c) Fonts (Google Fonts)**
- Retro phosphor: **VT323** (built from DEC VT320 glyphs, emulates beam-smear) for headers/glitch.
- Clean HUD readout rows: **Share Tech Mono**, **Space Mono**, or **JetBrains Mono** / **IBM Plex Mono** for the Linear-restraint version.

**(d) Signature devices**
1. **Scan reticle / crosshair** with corner brackets over the photo, plus a moving horizontal scan-line sweeping the image.
2. Readout rows: `NAME:`, `HANDLE:`, `PLATFORM:`, `BIO:` in mono with a blinking cursor and a "MATCH 98.2%" confidence line.
3. CRT texture: faint scanlines + subtle chromatic-aberration/glitch on hover, thin dot/line grid background.

## 5. PASSPORT / IDENTITY DOSSIER

**(a) References + the specific detail to steal**
- **Real passport data page (ICAO 9303)** -- the **MRZ**: two lines of 44 monospace characters (TD3) printed in **OCR-B**, using `&lt;` as the filler between fields. Steal this literally: render the name/handle encoded as an MRZ strip across the card bottom.
- **Guilloche security printing** -- fine interwoven wavy line patterns, microtext, and optically-variable ink that shifts color on tilt. Steal the guilloche as a faint background engraving + a rosette seal.
- **Papers, Please** -- desaturated bureaucratic palette (~3 muted shades per element), harsh contrast, and the thunk of a red inspection **stamp**. Steal the stamp + the cold officialdom.
- **Boarding passes / ID cards** -- steal the labeled field grid (SURNAME / GIVEN NAMES / TYPE / CODE) and a mugshot box with a portrait-tint.

**(b) Signature colors**
- Security paper tint: pale ecru `#EFE7D3` / `#E8E2CE` with a faint blue-green safety wash `#CFE0D8`.
- Guilloche line ink: desaturated navy `#2B3A55` and teal `#3E6E66`.
- Stamp red `#B4322A`; official gold-foil seal `#B8912E`.
- Papers-Please-cold neutral greys `#8A8D82`, `#3B3F3A`.

**(c) Fonts (Google Fonts)**
- MRZ line: **Cutive Mono** is the closest free Google Fonts stand-in for OCR-B (a genuinely free OFL "OCR-B" clone also exists if you want exact); **Nova Mono** as an alt.
- Field labels/data: **Archivo** or **Oswald** (stern institutional grotesque) for headers; **IBM Plex Mono** for data values.

**(d) Signature devices**
1. **MRZ strip** across the bottom -- two 44-char OCR-B lines with `&lt;&lt;&lt;` fillers encoding NAME/HANDLE.
2. Faint **guilloche** engraving + a circular rosette **seal** watermark behind the content.
3. A rotated red inspection **stamp** ("VERIFIED" / date met) and a labeled field grid with a portrait-tinted mugshot box.
